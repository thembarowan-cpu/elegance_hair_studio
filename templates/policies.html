Salon Policies & Frequently Asked Questions

Appointments can be booked online up to 30 days in advance. Please arrive 5–10 minutes early.

Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours before the appointment. Late cancellations may be subject to a fee (configurable by management).

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, your appointment may need to be rescheduled or shortened.

A deposit may be required for certain long services (e.g. full colouring or extensive braiding). This is configurable by the salon administrator.
